Directive: SASH-ENT-001. Inspection date: 14 November 2026. Rows S0-AGENTS, S0-OS, S1-EXE, S2-GESTURES, S2-STATE, S4-REPRO, and S5-ARM64 were rechecked against the source on 17 November 2026.
